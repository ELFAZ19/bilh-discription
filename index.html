Background

The mentorship problem is not only access. It is fragmentation, trust, and weak operational continuity.

Students struggle to find credible mentors, mentors struggle to run professional workflows, and institutions struggle to understand outcomes. Existing arrangements often lack legal clarity, reliable scheduling, documented progress, or auditable operations.

Discovery friction Quality mentors are hard to compare, shortlist, and trust.
Weak continuity Progress, notes, next steps, and accountability disappear between sessions.
Operational opacity Payments, disputes, approvals, and support actions remain hard to audit.
Institutional blind spots Organizations cannot easily see cohort health, adoption value, or delivery risk.
